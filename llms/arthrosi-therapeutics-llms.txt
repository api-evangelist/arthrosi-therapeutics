# Arthrosi Therapeutics

> Arthrosi Therapeutics, Inc. is a late-stage clinical biotechnology company founded in 2018
> and headquartered in San Diego, California. Its lead candidate, pozdeutinurad (AR882), is a
> once-daily oral next-generation URAT1 inhibitor in two fully enrolled replicate global Phase 3
> trials (REDUCE 1 and REDUCE 2) for progressive and tophaceous gout. Sobi (Swedish Orphan
> Biovitrum) completed its acquisition of Arthrosi on 9 February 2026.
>
> Arthrosi publishes NO public web API. There is no developer portal, no OpenAPI, no GraphQL
> endpoint, no AsyncAPI, no webhooks, no SDK or package on any registry, no MCP server and no
> A2A agent card. Arthrosi is a drug developer, not a software company; its only public digital
> surface is a WordPress corporate marketing site.
>
> Generated by API Evangelist from public sources on 2026-08-06. This is a third-party profile;
> API Evangelist does not operate Arthrosi Therapeutics software.

## Pipeline

- Pozdeutinurad (AR882) — oral selective URAT1 inhibitor for gout and tophaceous gout;
  two fully enrolled replicate Phase 3 trials, REDUCE 1 and REDUCE 2, pivotal data expected 2026.
- Registered clinical-trial records for AR882 are published by the sponsor on
  ClinicalTrials.gov, which is a third-party US National Library of Medicine service, not an
  Arthrosi API.

## Developer surface

There is none. Probed and confirmed on 2026-08-06:

- No `api.`, `docs.`, `developer.`, `developers.`, `portal.` or `app.` subdomain resolves in DNS.
- No GitHub organization (`github.com/arthrosi` → 404); no first-party repositories.
- No packages on npm, PyPI, or any other registry.
- No `/llms.txt`, `/openapi.json`, `/swagger.json`, `/api-docs`, or `/.well-known/*` document —
  every request is answered by the bot-challenge interstitial rather than a document.
